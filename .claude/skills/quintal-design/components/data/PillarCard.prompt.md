A pillar card. The `example` slot is what makes the pillar concrete — always fill it with a sentence the system would really send.

```jsx
<PillarCard
  index="01"
  title="Sono"
  description="Ajuda a entender e organizar padrões, sem virar polícia do sono."
  example="A Laura dormiu 40 minutos a menos. Vamos adaptar o restante do dia."
/>
```
